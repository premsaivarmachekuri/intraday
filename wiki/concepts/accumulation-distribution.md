# Accumulation & Distribution

> The invisible process by which smart money builds (accumulates) or unwinds (distributes) large positions inside price action — leaving volume fingerprints that reveal intent before price moves confirm it.

---

## Description

Accumulation and distribution are the two phases of institutional activity that bookend every significant price move. Understanding this cycle is essential for reading why volume behaves as it does.

**Accumulation:** Large operators quietly buy stock over weeks or months, often while price appears dull or sideways. They cannot buy everything at once without moving price against themselves, so they absorb shares gradually from weak hands.

**Distribution:** Once a large position is built and price has advanced (often driven partly by the accumulation itself), the large operators sell back into the public enthusiasm — disguising the selling behind rising volume and a seemingly healthy market.

> "Stocks are never distributed on the way up — they are distributed on the way down." — Livermore

**The trick:** Distribution often begins while the stock is still making new highs. The public sees rising prices and buys; the professional uses that buying to exit.

---

## Pioneer Perspectives

**Jesse Livermore:**
- Studied distribution patterns from his days as a pool operator (hired to distribute insider stock)
- Key distribution signal: **heavy volume + price fails to make new highs** = selling overpowering buying; the stock is being distributed
- Key accumulation signal: **light volume on reactions during an uptrend** = weak hands selling are easily absorbed; no real selling pressure
- End-of-move distribution: "heavy volume at the end of a long move = stocks going from strong hands (professionals) to weak hands (public)"
- The public views this heavy volume as "a vibrant, healthy market going through a normal correction" — it is actually a top
- Spike up on massive volume, then the stock rolls over and never makes a new high = blow-off top, distribution complete

---

## Synthesis

Accumulation and distribution leave volume signatures that are readable *before* the price move fully reveals itself. A stock that advances on declining volume is being accumulated quietly. A stock that "churns" — high volume, little price progress — is being distributed. This asymmetry is the edge: the professional activity leaves a trace that the alert trader can read.

---

## Key Rules

1. **Accumulation signal:** Light volume on pullbacks during uptrend — weak-hand sellers are easily absorbed; trend is healthy
2. **Distribution signal:** Heavy volume but price fails to make new highs — supply overwhelming demand; start reducing longs
3. **Blow-off top signal:** Sudden spike straight up on massive volume, then rollover — distribution complete; exit longs immediately
4. **Trapped-buyer supply:** Old resistance levels (prior highs) are where trapped buyers from the peak finally sell even — expect resistance there
5. **Breakout confirmation:** When a stock clears old highs on heavy volume, the overhanging trapped-buyer supply is exhausted — clear air above
