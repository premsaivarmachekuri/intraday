# Price & Volume Analysis

> The only two facts the market produces at end of day — everything else is opinion.

---

## Description

At the close of every trading day, all emotion, rumour, hope, fear, and noise is filtered away. What remains is: **price** (high, low, close) and **volume** (shares traded). Livermore built his entire system on these two data points. Everything else — earnings reports, analyst upgrades, CEO statements, macroeconomic data — is secondary noise that the tape will eventually reflect.

> "In the final analysis, at the end of a day of trading, everything boils down to only these two factors — price and volume. All of the emotion is gone, all the guesswork disappears." — Livermore

---

## Pioneer Perspectives

**Jesse Livermore:**

**On Price:**
- Price action is the graphic reflection of collective human emotion (fear, greed, hope, ignorance)
- Never too high to buy (if acting right); never too low to short (if acting right)
- A stock breaking to a new high has cleared all overhanging supply — clear air above
- A stock at a new low has trapped sellers dumping at any price — likely to continue lower
- Do not look at current price level as "high" or "cheap" — look at *direction* and *momentum*

**On Volume:**
- Volume changes are the most important alert signal
- A sudden change in volume (up or down) means something is afoot — do not ignore it
- Volume during accumulation: light on pullbacks, expanding on advances
- Volume during distribution: expanding on stalling prices, climactic at the top
- Volume at Pivotal Points: a Pivotal Point accompanied by 50%–500% volume expansion is confirmed; a Pivotal Point on thin volume is suspect

**Price + Volume Combinations:**

| Price | Volume | Interpretation |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| Rising | Rising | Confirmed uptrend — accumulation |
| Rising | Falling | Uptrend weakening — fewer participants |
| Falling | Falling | Normal correction — healthy |
| Falling | Rising | Distribution or panic — danger |
| Flat/churning | Very high | Distribution — exit signal |
| Spike up | Climactic | Blow-off top — exit longs |
| Breaking to new high | Heavy | Confirmed breakout — buy signal |

---

## Key Rules

1. Study price and volume together — neither alone is sufficient
2. Heavy volume + no new price high = distribution (exit longs)
3. Light volume + pullback = healthy correction (hold longs)
4. Volume surge at a Pivotal Point = confirmation (enter/add)
5. Volume surge with price stalling = warning (reduce/exit)
6. New high on heavy volume = legitimate breakout (buy)
7. Volume tells you WHAT is happening; price tells you WHERE — combine both
